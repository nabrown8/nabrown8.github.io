In the first half of the course, students will learn Python programming fundamentals and practice. Then in the second half, we'll look at various scientific applications of programming, like radioactivity modeling and medical imaging. The course caps off with a final project using programming to investigate a scientific question of students' choice!

### Course Information

There is no required textbook for this course. There are lots of Python resources available online and in print, however, which I strongly encourage students seek out for additional reading and practice. (Some examples to come at a later date.)
